Project Summary

Wyvern Warriors is a third-person aerial action game that blends fast-paced combat with high-pressure multitasking. Take control of a powerful wyvern and dominate the skies as you battle enemies, capture outposts, and protect the villages.

Intent vs. Outcome

With Wyvern Warriors the wanted to use the less traditional player traversal of flight to create a unique feeling of wonder for the player as they soared through the skies. Combining that with the constant tasks and enemies to create a fast-paced, exciting experience where the player always had something to do next. I believe we were successful in creating this experience, with an engaging movement system and a variety of tasks that kept players engaged.
